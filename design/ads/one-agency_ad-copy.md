# ONE Agency ad copy

Brand kit v2.0 · 2026-11-24. The copy for all 12 ads: the three approved sample ads and the ten creative concepts.

Every claim, figure and CTA is verbatim from oneagency.com (content pull of 2026-11-17) or a trim of a verbatim line, where words were removed and none added. Where an ad trims a line, the full line follows under **Trimmed from**. The only lines not from the site are eyebrows that name the service, and source or legal notes.

**Source files:** sample ads in `src/data/ad-copy.ts`, creative ads in `src/data/ad-creative.ts`. If this file and those disagree, the data files win.

**Accent word:** the one word in each headline set in Unbounded, in the solid accent colour.
